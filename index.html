About Signfiy

Signfiy is an innovative platform designed to bridge the communication gap between deaf and hard-of-hearing individuals and the hearing community. Our mission is to create a more inclusive world through technology.

Using cutting-edge artificial intelligence, computer vision, and natural language processing, we provide real-time translation services that make communication accessible and natural for everyone.
